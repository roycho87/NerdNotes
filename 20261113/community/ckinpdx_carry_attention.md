# Carry Attention: read the carried frames less (ckinpdx)

**Idea and first results by ckinpdx** ([ComfyUI-MMH3Tools](https://github.com/ckinpdx/ComfyUI-MMH3Tools), MIT). Posted on the Banodoco Discord on Nov 12 2026, published that night as **MiniMax H3 Carry Attention** (v0.109.0). Summarised here from his README, node docs and changelog.

## The idea

H3 has no cross-attention: the prompt, the reference pictures, the carried frames and the new frames all sit in one sequence. In a chain, the new frames lean heavily on the carried frames, which were generated by the last clip and have already drifted, so the drift is copied forward. Carry Attention adds a bias to the attention scores so the model **reads the carried frames less** and, optionally, **reads the reference pictures more**. The carried frames themselves stay clean; only how hard the model looks at them changes.

This fits what NerdNotes found with the keystone: drift comes from copying generated frames forward, and a source that never drifts makes a better anchor.

## His settings and results

A bias of b multiplies a part's share of attention by e^b.

| carry_bias | Share of attention | His result (one 32 s clip, 5 chunks, all 50 blocks, no anti-burn LoRA) |
|---|---|---|
| -1 (default) | about 0.37 | not reported |
| -2 | about 0.14 | joins held, slight bake left |
| -3, -4 | about 0.05, 0.02 | joins broke |

- `reference_bias` on its own (carry at 0) still left some burn. His conclusion: both halves do part of the work, turn the carry down and the references up.
- Turning the references up also pulls in the reference picture's pose, framing and background.
- Video only: the carried audio is not biased.
- His demo on Discord: a 1:42 talking head in chunks of a little over 5 seconds, with visibly better stability.
- It works with his Looping Sampler (`carry="mask"`) only, because the sampler publishes how many latents are carried.

## Geiru Attention Balance (experimental)

roycho's private experimental pack has a version of the same idea for Motion Context / Geiru Context chains: **Geiru Attention Balance**. It finds the carried frames from the noise mask instead of from a sampler, so it works with any noise-mask chain. Settings are strengths (1 = normal) rather than biases: context_strength, reference_strength, and keystone_strength for latent 0 when it holds clip 1's clean keystone. The way it locates the reference rows follows ckinpdx's MIT-licensed Reference Attention Probe.

**First result:** on the 30-clip beach benchmark, context_strength 0.7 (a bias of about -0.36, much milder than his default) made no difference: through clip 4 the darkening matched the plain chain almost exactly (-17.6 vs -17.7 brightness). The sweep from 0.4 down is in [test 19](../attention_sweep.md): the carry turned down alone didn't slow the darkening, but carry down + keystone cut it to about a third.

## Related

ckinpdx has also been working with frame 0 anchors since early August: `carry="keyframe"` (Aug 9) passes the previous chunk's tail as a guide anchored at frame 0, and the `dev/ref-carry` branch (Nov 10) passes it as a reference with one frame pinned at frame 0 to place the join. Both anchor to the previous chunk, for cleaner joins; the keystone anchors to clip 1, against drift.
